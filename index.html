About Us

Established in 2024, Kinance Limited is a premier debt recovery and accounts receivable management firm. The name Kinance beautifully unifies our local identity and mission: blending Kina (our national currency) and Finance to deliver powerful, localized financial protections.

We are a 100% wholly owned Papua New Guinean enterprise. This gives us an unmatched understanding of the local economic landscape, business cultures, and legislative frameworks required to resolve delicate accounts receivable dilemmas successfully.

Over our years of operation, we have proudly assisted numerous financially strained companies to securely recover stagnant debts, allowing them to reinvest in their operations, protect their payrolls, and thrive.

Our PNG Pride: As a homegrown business, we treat your business relations with extreme cultural awareness and professional respect, ensuring your corporate reputation remains intact throughout the collection lifecycle.

Our Capabilities

Commercial Debt Recovery

Specialized business-to-business (B2B) recovery intervention targeting overdue commercial invoices. We quickly resolve defaults to return valuable operating capital to your books.

Loan Debt Management

Comprehensive tracking, restructuring, and active recovery of personal, structured, or institutional loan portfolios that have fallen out of traditional terms.

Receivables Outsourcing

Allow our dedicated team to manage your early-stage aging accounts. We act as an extension of your accounts department to keep invoices paid on time, before they turn into debts.

Credit Management Consulting

We evaluate your existing credit policies, client onboarding risk profiles, and internal terms to insulate your company against future bad debt exposures.

Pre-Legal Dispute Resolution

Mediation strategies that find realistic solutions for non-payment, reducing the extreme expenses and stress related to court proceedings for both parties.

Skip Tracing & Investigations

Utilizing local networks and professional tools to locate missing debtors or verify hidden company assets across various provinces.
